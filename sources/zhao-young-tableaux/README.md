# Yufei Zhao — “Young Tableaux and the Representations of the Symmetric Group”

## Citation

Yufei Zhao, “Young Tableaux and the Representations of the Symmetric Group,” *Harvard College Mathematics Review* 2 (2008), 33–45.

- Author research page: https://yufeizhao.com/research/
- Author-hosted PDF: https://web.mit.edu/yufeiz/www/papers/youngtab.pdf

The PDF is dated May 2008.

## Why this source is here

This is a compact bridge source. Zhao starts with the combinatorial object and quickly gets to the representation-theoretic payoff: partitions and diagrams label irreducibles; tabloids generate permutation modules; polytabloids generate Specht modules; standard tableaux control bases/dimensions; and the branching rule and Young’s rule expose how representations change and decompose.

It is short enough that the later summary can be extremely explicit about every construction without turning into a book-length project.
