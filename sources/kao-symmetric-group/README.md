# Daphne Kao — “Representations of the Symmetric Group”

## Citation

Daphne Kao, “Representations of the Symmetric Group,” University of Chicago VIGRE/REU paper, dated September 1, 2010.

- University of Chicago PDF: https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~may/VIGRE/VIGRE2010/REUPapers/Kao.pdf

## Why this source is here

Kao’s paper takes a useful route from small concrete examples to the general theory. It begins with finite-group representation theory and character calculations for `S_3` and `S_4`, then describes the irreducibles of `S_n`, and finally points outward to Schur functors and representations of `GL(V)`.

That last step matters for this repository: it makes the symmetric group act not as an isolated object but as one half of the structure appearing on tensor powers, so tableaux become a mechanism for constructing other representations as well.
