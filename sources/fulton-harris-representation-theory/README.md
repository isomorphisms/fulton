## Why this source is here

Fulton–Harris is broader than this repository: its main arc is finite-dimensional representation theory leading into Lie groups and Lie algebras. That breadth is useful because it puts the symmetric-group story in the surrounding representation-theoretic language rather than treating tableaux as a combinatorics trick.

The immediately relevant portion is the finite-groups part, especially the chapter listed by Springer as **“Representations of S_d: Young Diagrams and Frobenius’s Character Formula”**, pp. 44–62. The preceding material on finite-group representations, characters, induced representations, and group algebras gives the machinery that makes that chapter readable.
