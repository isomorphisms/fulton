# Charlotte Chan — *Representation Theory of Symmetric Groups*

## Citation / provenance

Charlotte Chan, *Representation Theory of Symmetric Groups*, lecture notes, Hilary Term 2011. Chan states that she typed the notes for **Dr. Susanne Danz’s** Oxford course on Representation Theory of Finite Groups, offered as an extra-units Part C course in the 2010–2011 academic year.

- Institutional PDF: https://web.math.princeton.edu/~charchan/RepresentationTheorySymmetricGroupsNotes.pdf

## Why this source is here

These notes are a useful complement to the characteristic-zero introductions because their stated aim includes the modular theory. The contents run through:

- background representation theory;
- the symmetric group;
- Young diagrams, tableaux, and **tabloids**;
- permutation modules and Specht modules;
- character tables;
- induction and branching;
- the hook formula;
- RSK;
- modular representation theory;
- the modules `D^λ` and Specht modules in positive characteristic.

That makes the notes especially valuable for seeing the same combinatorial objects before and after semisimplicity fails.

Chan explicitly warns that the notes are unpolished and may contain errors. The later summary should preserve that caution and check important claims against the standard books rather than silently treating the notes as an infallible authority.
