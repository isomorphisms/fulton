## Why this source is here

Sagan’s book is unusually well matched to this repository because it deliberately approaches the symmetric group from three directions at once:

1. representation theory;
2. combinatorial algorithms;
3. symmetric functions.

Its second chapter goes directly through Young subgroups, tableaux and tabloids, dominance, Specht modules, standard bases, Garnir relations, Young’s natural representation, the branching rule, Kostka numbers, and Young’s rule. Later chapters connect that structure to RSK-type algorithms, hook-length phenomena, symmetric functions, differential posets, and chromatic symmetric functions.
